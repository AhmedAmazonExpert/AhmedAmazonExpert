## 🏆 Winner: Stack 2 — with conditions

**Stack 2 wins on structure and conversion efficiency**, but it needs Stack 1's storytelling depth and material differentiation to reach its full potential.

### Why Stack 2 Wins

1. **Pain-point-first sequencing.** Leading with freezer burn prevention (Slot 2) instead of a lifestyle hero shot is the correct choice for this product category. Shoppers searching for ice cream containers already know what they want — they need to be convinced this one solves their specific problems.

2. **Mobile-optimised scannability.** Amazon shoppers scroll fast on mobile. Stack 2's short, punchy headlines ("Stop Icy Freezer Burn", "Flexible Easy-Peel Lids") are readable at thumbnail size. Stack 1's longer copy blocks require zooming in.

3. **9 slots > 7 slots.** Amazon allows up to 9 image slots. Stack 2 uses all 9, giving it more surface area to address objections. The dedicated Cleaning (Slot 8) and Brand (Slot 9) slots address real purchase hesitations that Stack 1 bundles into other slots or omits entirely.

4. **Actionable content.** The "Press & Burp" seal technique (Slot 5) is genuinely useful and reduces negative reviews from improper use. Stack 1 has no instructional content.

5. **Production feasibility.** Stack 2's cleaner setups are faster and cheaper to produce, allowing quicker iteration and A/B testing.

### Where Stack 2 Falls Short (and needs Stack 1's DNA)

1. **Material storytelling is weak.** Stack 2 says "LFGB Silicone Lid. BPA-Free PP Body" but doesn't show the dual-material design visually. Stack 1's hang-tag, lid-peel reveal, and pinch test are superior at communicating what makes this product different.

2. **No emotional hooks.** "Durable Hybrid Construction" doesn't make anyone feel anything. Stack 1's "Your nails will thank you" and "NOT ANOTHER GENERIC TUB" create emotional resonance.

3. **No competitive contrast.** Stack 2 has no disposable-vs-reusable comparison. Stack 1's crushed carton visual is a proven conversion driver.

4. **Size reference is thin.** Stack 2 gives dimensions but no familiar objects for scale. Stack 1's milk carton and pint container comparisons are far more intuitive.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Lead with the pain point, not the product.** Freezer burn prevention should be in the first 2–3 slots for this category.
2. **Use all 9 slots.** Every empty slot is a missed objection-handling opportunity.
3. **Short headlines win on mobile.** Amazon's mobile app shows images at small sizes — headlines must be readable without zooming.
4. **Show the dual-material design early.** This is the product's core differentiator and the most misunderstood feature. Make it unmistakable in Slot 1.
5. **Teach proper use.** The "Press & Burp" seal technique reduces negative reviews and increases customer satisfaction.
6. **Include a competitive contrast.** A disposable-vs-reusable visual creates urgency and justifies the price premium.
7. **Compliance is non-negotiable.** Both stacks handle this well, but always hedge claims ("helps reduce" not "prevents") and specify "top-rack dishwasher safe" only.
